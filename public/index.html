How does booking here work?

The complete account and appointment workflow works in this preview. Its schedule is for demonstration. Requests are saved locally and do not book a real chair. Use Booksy for real availability.

Can I manage my appointment?

Yes. Sign in to see your requests, check their status and cancel an upcoming appointment. An owner can confirm or cancel requests from the dashboard.

Are the prices and barbers real?

They are taken from the public Booksy listing, checked October 10, 2026. Prices and staff may change. Photos are licensed illustrations until the owner supplies approved images.
