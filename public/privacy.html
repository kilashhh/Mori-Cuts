DATA & PRIVACY

Your information.

This independent preview is not operated by Mori Cuts. Use test details. No information is sent to the real business.

What this installation stores

Your name, email, a salted password hash, sessions and appointment details are stored in a local SQLite database. The installation owner can see customer names, emails and appointments. Passwords are not stored in plain text. Security rate limits temporarily use network addresses in memory.

Cookies and external services

A necessary HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. No analytics, advertising cookies or external fonts are loaded. Images are served locally. Maps and Booksy open only when you follow their links and have their own privacy policies.

Retention, access and removal

This demo has no automatic deletion policy or self-service account deletion. The installation owner must handle data access and removal requests through the server/database. Do not enter real customer data before the owner identifies a privacy contact, sets a retention policy and approves the deployment.

Accounts and recovery

Email verification, automatic password recovery, SMS, payment processing and email confirmations are not connected. Signed-in customers can change their passwords. Owner access can be recovered privately through the included owner console.

Real appointments

Demo requests do not reach the business. In an owner-approved live installation, the shop reviews requests and changes their status. This system does not synchronize with Booksy and must not be used in parallel without a process for avoiding conflicting bookings.
